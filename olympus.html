    Questions

    Everything you need to know before you begin.

    What is the pomodoro technique?

    You work in one focused block, usually 25 minutes, then rest briefly. After four blocks you take a longer break. Olympus changes the rhythm to match the god you choose.

    Which god should I pick?

    Zeus for daily discipline, Athena for hard problems, Apollo for creative work, Ares for short sprints when you are procrastinating, and Hypnos when you need to recover.

    Can I use my own music?

    Yes. Paste any YouTube video or playlist link into the Soundtrack card and press Load.

    Where is my data stored?

    Your tasks, intention and stats are saved in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
